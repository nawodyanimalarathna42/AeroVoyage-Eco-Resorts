Fleet & Marine Sanctuary Terminology

Amphibious Seaplane
An aircraft configured with retractable landing gear housed inside buoyant pontoons, granting full versatility to alight upon open marine lagoons as well as asphalt airstrips.
Biosphere Reserve
A UNESCO-recognized coastal or terrestrial sanctuary promoting conservation of biodiversity with sustainable eco-tourism through strict zero-emission protocols.
